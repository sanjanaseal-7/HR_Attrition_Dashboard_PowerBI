# 📊 HR Attrition Dashboard

# Project Overview:

I created this dashboard to analyse employee attrition patterns and identify key factors influencing employee turnover. The dashboard provides an interactive view of attrition across age groups, education backgrounds, salary slabs, years at the company, and job roles, helping HR teams understand workforce trends and identify areas that may require attention.

# Tools & Skills:

• Power BI | Excel | Power Query | Data Cleaning | Data Modelling | Data Visualization | Dashboard Design

# Key Insights Covered:

• Total Employees: 1,470

• Employee Attrition: 237 employees

• Overall Attrition Rate: 16.1%

• Average Employee Age: 37 years

• Average Salary: 6.5K

• Average Years at Company: 7.0 years

# Business Insights:

• 16.1% attrition rate indicates a significant employee retention challenge.

• Employees aged 26–35 show the highest attrition, making them a key retention segment.

• Lower salary bands (up to 5K) account for the majority of attrition cases.

• Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives have the highest attrition among job roles.

• Life Sciences employees represent the largest share of attrition by education.

Attrition is highest during the early years of employment, highlighting the importance of employee engagement and retention strategies.
